NFC stickers

iPhone can't scan NFC inside a web app — stickers still work: hold one near the top of the phone and its link opens the item or shelf directly, no app needed.

Writing a sticker

Write stickers right here in the app: open any item or shelf, tap Write sticker, then hold a blank NTAG sticker to the back of the phone. Quick Add offers the same button right after an item is added, and a Copy sticker link button is on those sheets too if you ever need the link on a clipboard.

This phone's browser can't write NFC — an Apple limit on every web app — but writing takes about 30 seconds per sticker, one time only: (1) on the item or shelf, tap Copy sticker link; (2) open the free NFC Tools app (by wakdev) → Write → Add a record → URL/URI → paste the link → OK; (3) tap Write and hold the sticker to the top of the phone. After that, stickers open by tapping — no app needed to read them.
